Lean companion of the Abstract Algebra I course, proving theorems in the notes.

Note added in Feb 26 - With the help of Aristotle (https://aristotle.harmonic.fun/), many of the
theorems proved in class become very formalizable. There are a few things one can take advantage
of the codes:

(1) Copy and paste the codes to your favorite LLM, and let them explain what the proofs are
about in human language.

(2) By moving along the proofs from line to line, one can see how different tactics applied in the 
proofs affect the goal status. This will give us a better understanding of different mathlib 
tactics.

(3) The proofs generated by aristotle can be hard to follow. There are often easier ways to
make the proof more comprehensible to human being. Give it a try!
